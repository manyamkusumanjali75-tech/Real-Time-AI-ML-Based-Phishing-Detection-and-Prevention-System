🛡️ Real-Time AI/ML-Based Phishing Detection & Prevention System

Intelligent Cybersecurity Solution for Detecting Phishing Websites, Malicious URLs and Online Threats in Real Time

🎯 Major Phishing Threats

  1. 🎣 Phishing Websites attempting to steal user information.
  2. 💀 Malicious Links redirecting users to harmful websites.
  3. 🔒 Credential Theft through fake login pages.
  4. 🌐 Spoofed Domains imitating trusted websites.
  5. 🎭 Social Engineering attacks targeting users.
  6. ⚠️ Zero-Day Threats exploiting unknown vulnerabilities.
  7. 📡 Man-In-The-Middle (MITM) attacks.
  8. 💻 Malware Distribution via suspicious URLs.
  9. 🔑 Identity Theft and confidential data leakage.
  10. 🕵️ Cyber Fraud leading to financial loss.

🚀 AI/ML Detection Workflow

  • 📝 Step 1 – User enters website URL.
  • 🔍 Step 2 – Protocol extraction and URL parsing.
  • 🔐 Step 3 – Safe protocol verification.
  • ✅ Supported Protocols: HTTP, HTTPS, FTP, SFTP.
  • ⚠️ Unsafe Protocols: File, JavaScript, Data, Unknown.
  • ⚙️ Step 4 – AI/ML feature extraction and analysis.
  • 📊 Step 5 – Threat score and phishing prediction.
  • 🛡️ Step 6 – Security recommendations generated.
  • 💾 Step 7 – Result stored in database.
  • 📜 Step 8 – History and reporting dashboard.

📌 Introduction

The Real-Time AI/ML-Based Phishing Detection and Prevention System is designed to identify suspicious websites before users become victims of cyber attacks. By leveraging machine learning algorithms, the system performs intelligent URL analysis and provides real-time threat predictions.
